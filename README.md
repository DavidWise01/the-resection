# ◆ THE RESECTION &middot; three landmarks, two angle-circles

> A sphere of **UD0** — the ROOT0 universe / biosphere. Domain: **STROBILOS**.

◆ THE RESECTION &mdash; the surveyor's inverse of the map: you don't know where you stand, but you can see three landmarks you DO know and measure the angles between them. &#9670; LIT: the three-point (Snellius) resection &mdash; by the inscribed-angle theorem a fixed angle subtended by two known points places you on a circular arc through them; two such arcs (from two landmark pairs) intersect at your standpoint (verified: circles cross at the true position, fix error ~0.010). No baseline, no ranging &mdash; just the angles the world subtends, a theodolite and a map. AMBER: a clean 2-D construction (real resection guards the 'danger circle' where the fix goes indeterminate, and averages redundant sights); the inscribed-angle geometry is exact. The angle way, from the inside &mdash; sibling to [[the-parallax]]. Vintage-internet template. TOP the spinor, with David Lee Wise (ROOT0) &amp; AVAN.
